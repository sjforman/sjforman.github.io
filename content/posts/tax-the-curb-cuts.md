Proposal: after repealing mandatory off-street parking requirements, go a step further and impose a significant annual fee on curb cuts. 

<!--more-->

They're mobility and safety-eroding, bikelane-preventing privatizations of scarce public curb space. And many are used only to provide easy access for private vehicles to the space *in front of the garage* where the vehicle doesn't even fit. They shouldn't be free, and we should incentivize removing them.
